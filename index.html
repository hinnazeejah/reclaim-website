A little care goes a long way

Our code of conduct.

Everyone deserves to feel welcome, respected, and safe at RECLAIM. These expectations apply to attendees, speakers, sponsors, volunteers, and hosts, in person and in event-related online spaces.

01 / Make room

Lead with respect.

Welcome people across backgrounds and experiences. Listen, make space for others, and respect names, pronouns, and perspectives. Discrimination, harassment, threats, and unwanted sexual attention have no place here.

02 / Ask first

Respect boundaries.

Ask before taking or sharing someone’s photo, recording a conversation, or sharing their contact details. Respect a “no” and anyone’s choice to step away. Keep networking friendly and free of pressure.

03 / Take care

Care for the space.

Follow venue guidelines and directions from the event team. Keep shared spaces accessible, treat people and property with care, and help create a day everyone can enjoy.

04 / Look out for each other

Speak up. We’re here.

If something feels wrong or you witness a concern, reach out to a host. Respect anyone who raises a concern; retaliation is not acceptable. Hosts may ask someone to stop a behavior or leave the event.

Need support?

Find Hinna, Mariyam, or Aimen at the event, or email Hinna or Aimen. You don’t need to handle a concern alone. We’ll listen and work with you on next steps.

A few things to know

Glad you
asked.

A little clarity before we connect.

Is RECLAIM affiliated with Grace Hopper Celebration?

No. RECLAIM is an independent, community-organized event and is not affiliated with GHC or AnitaB.org.

Where exactly is it?

At UC Irvine in Irvine, California. Check your RSVP updates for the exact meeting point. Rideshare recommended.

Is there parking available?

Parking is very limited and only available by permit. We recommend ubering!

Who can come?

Students, early-career professionals, and women across every corner of tech.

Is it free?

Yes! RECLAIM is free to attend. Save your spot and come connect with the community.

I already booked travel for GHC. Is this for me?

Yes, that's exactly who we built this for.

How do I become a speaker or sponsor?

Use the Get Involved section or email us. We'd love to hear from you.
